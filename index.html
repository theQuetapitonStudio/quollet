What is QNK-BASE?

QNK-BASE is the base denomination of QNK, the currency used throughout the Quollet ecosystem. Quollet is part of Quenoky World and provides digital products, services and an internal economy built around QNK.

Where can QNK be used?

QNK can be used within Quollet for products, services and transactions. The Quollet Shop and Quollet Crypto Shop are part of the ecosystem, with additional uses planned for future Quollet projects.
